# Miravoya — Interactive Travel Explorer, Trip DNA, Passport & Roadmap Studio

An impressive, zero-dependency, modern travel web application covering **Tamil Nadu (TN)**, **India**, and **Other Countries** with exclusive interactive features not found on ordinary travel booking sites.

## Exclusive Signature Innovations

- **🧬 Smart Trip DNA Matchmaker** — Dial in your region (TN / India / World), max budget, trip length, vibe, and travel pace to compute live `% Match` scores across 25 destinations and load your #1 soulmate roadmap in one click.
- **🛂 Collectible Digital Travel Passport + PNG Export** — Slam realistic custom-ink airport stamps onto your open passport pages as you visit destinations, level up your Explorer Rank, and download a custom-rendered **PNG Passport Souvenir Card**.
- **🗓️ 12-Month “When to Go” Peak Weather Heatmap** — Tap any month (`Jan`–`Dec`) to instantly see every Tamil Nadu, India, and international destination in peak weather perfection.
- **🌊 Built-in Ambient Travel Soundscapes** — Pure Web Audio synthesized *Ocean Waves*, *Alpine Wind*, and *Monsoon Rain* ambience right in the floating dock while you plan.
- **✨ 6 Living Atmospheric Themes** — Switch seamlessly between *Golden Horizon*, *Aurora Borealis*, *Sahara Dusk*, *Monaco Grand Luxe* (Royal Emerald & 24K Gold), *Tokyo Cyber Neon*, and *Kyoto Twilight* with frosted glass cards and a 3D holographic Digital Boarding Pass.

## Core Travel Platform Features

- **3-Scope Geographic Explorer** — Divided cleanly into **Tamil Nadu (8 places)**, **India (7 icons)**, and **Other Countries (10 wonders)** with category sub-filters (*Hills*, *Beaches*, *Heritage*, *City*, *Adventure*, *Food*) and a **Full Details Travel Guide** modal (Top Landmarks, Local Food, How to Reach, Pro Tips).
- **Interactive SVG World Route Map** — Animated curved golden flight arcs with a gliding flight beacon.
- **Multi-Modal Booking Studio + Perforated Digital Boarding Pass** — Flights, Stays, Experiences & Rail/Cars across 5 live currencies (`INR`, `USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `JPY`).
- **Side-by-Side Destination Comparator** — Compare up to 3 cities live with animated meter bars and automatic *Best Value* highlight.
- **Interactive Day-by-Day Trip Roadmap & Toolkit** — Editable timeline builder with `.md` export, Budget & Split-Bill calculator, Smart Packing Checklist, FX Converter, and 8-language Local Phrasebook (including Tamil & Hindi).

## Quick Start & GitHub Pages

1. Open `index.html` directly in any browser, or run locally:
   ```bash
   python3 -m http.server 8098 --bind 127.0.0.1
   ```
2. Push this folder to GitHub and enable **Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch (`main` / root)**.
